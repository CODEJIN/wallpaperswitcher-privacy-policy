WallpaperSwitcher(이하 "앱")는 Wi-Fi 연결 상태와 폴더블 기기의 접힘/펼침 상태에 따라 사용자가 지정한 폴더의 이미지를 배경화면(홈/잠금화면)으로 자동 전환해주는 개인용 유틸리티 앱입니다. 이 개인정보처리방침은 앱이 접근하는 정보와 그 사용 방식을 설명합니다.

1. 앱은 서버로 어떤 데이터도 전송하지 않습니다

이 앱은 완전히 오프라인으로 동작합니다. 자체 서버, 백엔드, 분석(analytics) SDK, 광고 SDK를 포함하지 않으며, 앱이 다루는 모든 데이터는 사용자의 기기 안에만 저장되고 외부로 전송되지 않습니다. 개발자를 포함한 어느 누구도 사용자의 데이터를 원격으로 수집·조회·판매·공유하지 않습니다.

WallpaperSwitcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-22

WallpaperSwitcher (the "App") is a personal utility app that automatically switches the home/lock screen wallpaper to an image from a folder you choose, based on which Wi-Fi network you're connected to and whether your foldable device is folded or unfolded. This policy explains what information the App accesses and how it is used.

1. The App does not send any data to a server

The App works entirely offline. It has no backend server, no analytics SDK, and no advertising SDK. All data the App handles is stored only on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. Nobody, including the developer, can remotely collect, view, sell, or share your data.

2. Information the App accesses on your device

3. Data retention and deletion

All data described above is kept only in the App's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes this data as well. The event log and excluded-image list can be cleared or restored directly from within the App.

4. Third-party sharing

The App does not provide, sell, or share any data with third parties, and includes no advertising or tracking SDKs.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
